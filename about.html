Our Mission

To provide education, nutrition, and healthcare to underprivileged children, creating sustainable change and brighter futures.

Our Vision

A world where every child has access to quality education, proper nutrition, and the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Transparency, and Empowerment guide every decision we make and every life we touch.

Our Journey

Founded in 2020 by Ivan, Hope Smile Foundation began with a simple act of kindness - providing school supplies to children in a rural village. Today, we've grown into a organization impacting the health and education support to children .

Our journey has been fueled by the dedication of volunteers, the generosity of donors, and the resilience of the children we serve. Each smile we help create motivates us to do more.
